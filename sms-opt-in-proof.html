SMS Opt-In Verification

This page documents the consent step used for the Hope & Caleb Wedding SMS program. The live RSVP is invitation-gated to protect guest information, so the consent control appears after a guest has found their own invitation.

Guest consent journey

  1. A guest visits the RSVP page and enters the first and last name of an invited guest.
  2. After the invitation group is found, the guest completes their RSVP.
  3. The guest may optionally enter a mobile number and separately select the unchecked SMS consent box shown below.

Entering a mobile number and consenting to texts are never required to RSVP.

Consent records

When a guest actively selects this checkbox, the RSVP system records the mobile number, opt-in status, and consent timestamp. Only consented contacts are eligible for program messages.
