Why It Felt Off

The mismatch was coming from a separate planner shell.

The rounded pills, boxed top bar, and card stack were not coming from the celebration page at all. They were hard-coded in this app template, which made the planner homepage feel disconnected from the site you already liked.

Next Build

Now we can evolve one site instead of juggling two styles.

The next step is to keep lifting pieces from the celebration page into the wedding version: public-facing content first, then planner tools where they support the same story and layout.
